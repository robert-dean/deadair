Library › Tracks opens a record in a drawer beside the list when you click its row: its copies, its measurement, when it has aired, your rating, a Request button that asks for it the way a listener would, and a link to the full record page. Each row now carries one readiness mark (Ready, Not measured or Suspended) instead of a badge per fact, and "Not fetched", which is how most of a library looks, is drawn as quiet text rather than in colour. Rating a record now also refreshes its own page.
