Programme has a Festivals tab: add a festival with its dates, where it is and its bill (headliners in their own box, one act per line, and an act's day after a comma), and see whether each is still to come, on now or over. A block's editor has a "Built around a festival" picker, which offers the festivals still to come and keeps one a block already names.
