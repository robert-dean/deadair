The nightly pass that thinks up your characters' stories now looks only at the station's own music: its library, your ratings, similar artists and the charts. It used to be offered every tool the station has, including web search, the news, the weather and what happened on today's date, so text from the open web could end up in a story, and on a character allowed to grow by itself be kept without anybody reading it. A detail added to an existing story by such a character is now checked against the character's sheet before it is kept, as new stories and new parts of a story already were.
