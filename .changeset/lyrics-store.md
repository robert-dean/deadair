The station can keep the words of its records. With `lyrics.fetch` on (it is off by default), a walk asks every lyrics plugin for each record's words and line timings, most-needed records first, and stores one answer per source. Nothing serves the text: the station reads it to know when the singing starts, and never says it. Lyrics sources show up on the Providers page beside the other jobs more than one plugin can do.
