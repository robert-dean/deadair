The station keeps festivals: a name, the first and last day, where it is, and who is on the bill, with each act's day and whether they headline when the bill says. `GET`, `POST`, `PUT` and `DELETE /festivals` read and write them, and each answers whether a festival is still to come, on now or over on the station's calendar.
