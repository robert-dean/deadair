A show can carry a mix flow: its music rules as nodes and wires, which the station enforces on every record it chooses for the show instead of hoping the model keeps count. A flow can block bands, split records by their band's main genre (or artist, decade, mood, hour, how far through the show), limit what passes a node to a share of each hour or show or to a number, keep two of a kind from airing back to back, feed a limit a different share after a given hour, and require a record once the show is far enough in: one of a list, aired longest ago first, or any record whose band's main genre matches. Records it would hold are left out of each refill, and the model is told what each limit has left so it stops naming them. A listener's request, and what a request show plays after one, is never judged by a flow and never counted against it. A schedule slot and a broadcast put on air by hand take `mixFlow`; a save that leaves it out keeps what the slot had, and a flow with no nodes clears it. There is no editor in the console yet. A new setting, off by default, lets a model name a main genre for bands that have none, so their records are sorted and counted too.
