| `id`            | `string`                                                                                                                         | Yes      | Unique in the flow                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `kind`          | `'picks' \| 'onAir' \| 'block' \| 'split' \| 'limitShare' \| 'limitCount' \| 'noRepeat' \| 'merge' \| 'require' \| 'timeSwitch'` | Yes      | `picks` is what the station proposes and `onAir` what may air. `block` keeps the named bands off. `split` sends each record down the first branch it matches. `limitShare` and `limitCount` hold records past an amount of what has passed through them. `noRepeat` holds a record when the last record aired also passed through it. `merge` joins wires. `require` adds a record when due. `timeSwitch` is a number that changes at an hour |

A number wired into its input 1 wins over this                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
