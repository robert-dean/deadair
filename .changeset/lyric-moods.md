With `lyrics.moods` on (off by default), a model judges what mood each record is in, as shares of love, happiness, comfort, sadness, loneliness, anger and fear, reading the record's lyrics and, where the station can search the web, what is written about it. It runs in the background at the lowest priority, and a record it cannot place is recorded as such rather than guessed. The Library page counts the records judged so far.
