# Code of conduct

This applies everywhere the project is: issues, pull requests, Discussions, and the community
catalogue's repository.

## What is expected

- Be decent. Disagree with the idea, never the person.
- Assume the other person is running a station on their own time, as you are.
- Answer the question that was asked. "Read the docs" is not an answer; a link to the paragraph is.
- Credit what you share. A character, a plugin or a clock somebody else wrote stays theirs.

## What is not tolerated

- Harassment, slurs, or attacks on anybody's identity, in any form and in any language.
- Sexual content or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Posting somebody else's private information, including a station address they did not publish.
- Sharing music, keys or accounts that are not yours to share, or asking others to.
- Spam, and posts whose only purpose is to sell something.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or discussion, which reaches the maintainer
and GitHub both. Reports are read, and the person reported is not told who made one.

## What happens

The maintainer decides, and may edit or remove anything, lock a thread, or block an account from
the project. Most things end with a note asking somebody to stop; a few end sooner.
