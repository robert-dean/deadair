The desk is laid out in two columns. What is on air stays pinned on the left while you read the running order: the record, how far in it is, then Skip, Stop or Start, Go live and the speaker menu together, with the live input opening inside the same card. Keeping the station quiet is one control (As planned, Quiet 3, 10, Rest) that shows its countdown while a quiet spell runs, and Needs you sits underneath in a compact list. The running order on the right reads as a clock: each row says when it airs, every hour gets a heading, breaks show as small dots on the rail, and the last stop is when the order runs dry, with Extend beside it. A time marked with a + is the earliest a row can air, because something before it has no length the station could count.
