Set **Language** (`stream.language`) to something other than English, such as `de` or `fr`, and the presenter writes in it. Breaks, phone-ins and other productions are asked for in that language, while titles and names stay as written, and a persona generated from a description has its sample lines, catchphrases, stories and phrasings written in it too. An empty setting, or any English tag, leaves the station exactly as it was.
