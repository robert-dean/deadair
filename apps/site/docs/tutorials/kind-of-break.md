# Invent a kind of break

By the end of this page the station says something of your own invention between records: here, a
two-line poem about the moment at a quarter to every hour, in the voice of whoever is presenting.
You write a brief rather than code, the station's model writes each one when its time comes, and the
result can be exported and given to another station.

[Kinds of break you write](../features/breaks.md#kinds-of-break-you-write) explains what such a kind
can and cannot do.

**You need** a station on air with a voice, and a model writing the breaks
([Give the station a model](./connect-a-model.md)): **Let a model write the talk breaks** on under
**Settings → Words**. A kind you write has no phrasings underneath it, so without a model it never
airs.

## 1. Write the brief

Open **Voice → Kinds of break** and press **New kind of break**.

- **Name**: `A poem about the moment`. Only the console shows it.
- **On the format clock as**: `poem`. Lowercase words joined by hyphens. A band names the kind by this
  word, so it cannot be changed after you save.
- **What it is for**, written to the presenter as you would brief a person:

  ```
  Two unrhymed lines about this moment on the station: the hour, the record that just ended,
  the mood of the show. Nothing else, no introduction and no sign-off. If nothing comes to
  mind, say nothing.
  ```

- **Rules of its own**, one per line: `Never more than two lines.` and `No rhymes.`
- **While writing one, the presenter sees**: tick **The record that has just finished**. Leave the
  rest off for now; each one you tick gives the poem more to work with, and more to drift into.

Leave **Switched on** off and press **Save**. The kind is listed as **Off**, with **No band yet**.

## 2. Read it back, then switch it on

Open it again and read the brief as the presenter will. The station's own rules are added on top of
yours whatever you write: the presenter stays in character, names no record it was not shown and
states nothing it was not told. You cannot write those away, so there is no need to repeat them.

Switch on **Switched on** and save. The list now reads **On**. If it reads **Not airing**, the model
writing the breaks or the voice is switched off, and nothing airs until both are on.

## 3. Put it on the format clock

Open **Programme → Today**, find **Format clock** and press **Add band**.

1. Write `poem` as the **Sort of break**.
2. Choose **every hour at** and set **Minutes past the hour** to `45`.
3. To keep it to the evening shows, pick days under **on** or leave them empty for every day.
4. Save.

Back on **Voice → Kinds of break**, the poem now says **On 1 band**.

## 4. Hear it

At a quarter to the hour, when the record playing then ends, the presenter says the poem. **Voice →
What it said** lists it like every other break, and any the
model declined and why.

Not what you meant? Change **What it is for** and save. The next one is written from the new
brief. One already in the running order can be written afresh with **Write it again** on its row on
the Desk.

## 5. Give it to another station

On **Voice → Kinds of break**, the export button on the poem's row saves it as a file, and **Export
all** saves every kind you have written in one.

On the other station, press **Import** and choose the file. Before anything is written it shows what
it would do with each kind: **New**, **Keep mine** or **Replace** for one it already has, and **Not
allowed** for a word that station uses itself. Press **Import**. Everything imported arrives switched
off, so read it back and switch it on there, then give it a band on that station's clock.

## If it goes wrong

- **The band says nothing can produce it.** The kind is off, or the station has no model writing the
  breaks, or no voice. A kind you write needs all three.
- **Saving is refused over the word.** The station already has a kind of break by that name, such as
  `news`, `weather` or `talk`. Choose another word.
- **The poem names records, or rambles.** Say what you do not want in **Rules of its own**, and tick
  less under **While writing one, the presenter sees**.
- **Some quarter-hours pass with no poem.** The model declined: nothing it wrote passed the station's
  checks. **Voice → What it said** says why. A brief that tells it when to say nothing makes that the
  usual reason.
- **Delete is refused.** A band on the clock still names the kind. Remove the band first.
