The backup news reader, used when no model writes the bulletin, reads more like a newsreader. Its "Now, …" handover names the artist as well as the record ("Now, The Cure with Pictures of You."). A Title Case headline is read in ordinary sentence case, keeping acronyms, brand names and any name the story itself capitalises. A publisher's doubled small word ("amidst an an ongoing dispute") is said once. And a story with only a teaser to read is left out when other stories have their article, so a bulletin no longer stops on "New music genre just dropped."
