A bundled LRCLIB plugin finds the words of each record and the timing of each line, matched strictly by artist, title, album and length with no looser second try. It asks nothing until lyrics are switched on, can be pointed at a copy of LRCLIB you run yourself, and fails loudly rather than quietly when the service refuses it.
