When the presenter talks over a record, the music now drops to a set level under the voice instead of by a set amount. A quiet intro only dips a little and a loud chorus comes down further, so the voice sits the same distance above the music either way. If a record kicks in while the presenter is still talking, the music is pulled back under the voice within a second. A record at the station's usual level ducks exactly as it did before.
