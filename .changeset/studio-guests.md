A persona can be a **guest**: somebody who drops by the studio for a short chat with the host, rather than ringing in like a caller. A guest never presents the station, has no phrasings, and can be tied to the hosts whose shows they visit, as a caller is. The Personas page has New guest and lists guests in a group of their own. Refusing to put a character on air now says what kind it is, so a newsreader is no longer told it is a caller. A **visit** is a new kind of production: the host welcomes one guest into the studio for a short conversation, the guest is written as somebody in the room rather than on a telephone, and the host thanks them for dropping by at the end. A visit is cast from the guests tied to that host (or to nobody), least recently heard first, and is failed with a reason, rather than aired as the host alone, when the station has no guests.
