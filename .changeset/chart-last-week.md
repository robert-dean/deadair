A chart entry can carry the record's position in the edition before (`lastWeek`), on the plugin SDK's `ChartEntry`, the `ChartRecord` contract and the placing a chart pick carries into the running order. The station's own Top 40 fills it, and a chart plugin may. On a chart show the presenter is told how the record moved from its two positions alone ("up from number 12 on the chart before"), never the distance between them, and the break guard counts last week's position as a figure the break was shown.
