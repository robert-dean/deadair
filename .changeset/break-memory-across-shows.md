A presenter no longer opens a new show on the line the last one just closed with. What the station has already said was remembered only within the current broadcast, so a stand-down or a restart wiped it while listeners were still tuned in. A show that has only just started now also avoids what the station said in the hour before it. A station coming back after a quiet night still starts clean, and the records a presenter may refer back to are still only the ones this show played.
