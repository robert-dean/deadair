Two new settings under Settings, Station tell every presenter about the station itself. **About the station** is who it is, in a sentence or two: what it plays, who it is for. **What is going on at the station** is anything true right now, like an anniversary, a fundraiser or a move. Both are given to every break as background, after the station's name and before the presenter's character. The second is offered as something to know rather than to announce, so it comes up where it fits instead of in every link. Left empty, breaks are written exactly as before.
