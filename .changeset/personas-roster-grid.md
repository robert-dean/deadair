Voice, Characters is easier to scan. Characters are a grid of cards, each with its portrait or initials, its badges, two lines of who it is, its voice with a play button, and what it wrote, declined or failed in the last day. Chips narrow the roster to hosts, to callers and guests, or to the ones that spoke in the last 24 hours, and the host of the next scheduled block is badged Next. A strip at the top always says who is presenting, and the presenter name and story wait now open from its "Settings for presenters" button. Export all, Import and Restore built-ins are under More.
