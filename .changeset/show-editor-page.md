A show now opens on a page of its own instead of a dialog over Programme, so it can be reloaded, linked to and left with the back button. Press a block on the Timetable, its pencil on Today or its row on Specials, or click an empty hour for a new one. The page is in parts (When, Who presents it, Music, What it says, Sound and Repeat rules) with a list of them down the side, and on a wide screen a card beside the form says how the show will sound as it stands. If you have changed something and not saved, leaving the page asks first; saving or deleting takes you back to the tab you came from. Programme's Today tab also gains the whole day on one line, with each show where it falls and a line at now, and the current hour drawn against the format clock: what has aired, what is still to come, and the minutes the clock's bands want.
