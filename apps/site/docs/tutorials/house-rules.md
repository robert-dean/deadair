# Keep records off the air

By the end of this page the station never plays what you do not want it to: one record, everything by
one artist, a whole genre, live recordings, or anything explicit. Each of these is absolute. No
playlist, chart, album or listener's request gets round it.

[The programme](../features/programme.md#your-opinion-and-the-rules-nothing-gets-round) explains the
rules in full.

**You need** a station on air with music. Nothing here needs a model.

There are three tools, from the narrowest to the widest:

| To keep off the air | Use |
| --- | --- |
| One record, one album, or everything by one artist | A dislike |
| A whole kind of record: a genre, a tag, live recordings | A never-play rule |
| Explicit records | The explicit-content setting |

## 1. Dislike what you never want to hear

A dislike is the quickest: press it while the record is on.

- **On the Desk**, in the running order, set the record's rating to **Never play**.
- **In the Library**, open a record, an album or an artist and do the same. Disliking an artist keeps
  every record of theirs off the air, including the ones they only guest on.
- **From your phone**, signed in as the operator, use the record's rating on Now playing
  ([Run the station from your phone](./phone-remote.md)).

A dislike takes effect at once. Records already planned come out of the running order straight away,
rather than waiting for it to be rebuilt.

A dislike always wins. Like a song by an artist you disliked and it still does not play. To undo one,
set the rating back to **No opinion**.

## 2. Refuse a whole kind of record

For "no Christmas music", "no rap in the mornings" or "no live versions", dislikes are too narrow. Use
a never-play rule.

1. Open **Programme → Leans and rules** and find **Never play**.
2. Choose what the rule refuses:
   - **Genre** refuses that genre and any kind of it. A rule for `Punk` also refuses Punk Rock. A rule
     for `Rap` does not refuse Trap.
   - **Tag** refuses a record carrying exactly that tag.
   - **Version: Live recordings** refuses a record whose title or album says it is live, such as
     "War Pigs - Live" or anything from "Live in Nottingham". "Live Wire" still plays.
3. Leave the limits empty for a rule that always holds. Or limit it:
   - **From (MM-DD)** and **To (MM-DD)** for a season. `12-01` to `01-06` runs over the new year.
   - **From hour** and **Until hour** for a window of the day. `22` to `6` runs over midnight.
   - **Only in these modes**, or **Only during these blocks**, to hold it only for some shows.
4. Press **Add rule**.

The rule applies from the next record the station chooses. Records already queued stay.

![Leans and rules: a lean toward some genres, and the never-play rules with the form for a new one](/img/console/schedule.rules.webp)
*Fig. 1. **Programme → Leans and rules**, with one rule already in force: no live recordings.*

**A record nobody tagged is never refused by a rule.** A rule matches the genres and tags the station
holds for a record, so it is only as good as those. A record that slips through can still
be disliked.

### A worked example: Christmas, but only in December

Add a **Never play** rule for the genre `Christmas`, from `12-27` to `11-30`. It holds for most of
the year, running over the new year, and lifts for December. **Leans and rules** shows it as **Not
now** while it is lifted.

Pair it with a [special](../features/programme.md#specials) in December if you want a Christmas show
as well. A special's playlist cannot get round a rule that is holding, so keep the special inside the
weeks the rule is lifted.

## 3. Keep it clean

1. Open **Settings → Rotation** and find **Explicit content**.
2. Choose:
   - **Play the original version**, the default.
   - **Prefer a clean version where there is one.** It leans toward the clean copy, and still plays the
     original when that is all there is.
   - **Only play records marked clean.** Strict.
3. Save.

**"Only play records marked clean" can empty the station.** Explicit or clean is a label your music
provider puts on a copy, and most providers mark nothing. On their libraries the strict setting plays
nothing, and the activity feed says so. Try **Prefer a clean version** first.

Either of the last two also keeps the presenter's own words clean, even for a character given extra
room.

## 4. Check it

**Programme → Leans and rules** lists every rule with **In force** or **Not now**, so a seasonal rule
shows whether it is holding today. The **Library** shows each record's, album's and artist's rating.

When a rule or a dislike refuses something you put on yourself, such as a playlist, the record is left
out and the rest plays in order.

## If it goes wrong

- **A record I disliked played anyway.** It may be a different recording of the song, such as a
  remaster or a live version, which the library holds as a record of its own. Dislike that one too, or
  the artist.
- **A rule did not stop a record.** The record carries no genre the rule matches. Open it in the
  Library to see its genres, and dislike it if it has none.
- **Nothing plays with "Only play records marked clean".** Your provider does not mark records. Use
  **Prefer a clean version where there is one**.
