A new install now seeds a second off-air news category, "Programmes and promos", beside "Shopping and sponsored". It keeps a publisher's own show listings, puzzles and episode promos out of bulletins (a station read "the Wait Wait guest for October 10 is Margo Price" as news). It matches listing phrasing such as "guest host", "this episode" and "puzzlemaster"; add the names of the shows your feeds carry to finish it. An existing station can add the category on the news topics page. The news writer is also told to leave out a listing for a show, a puzzle or an episode.
