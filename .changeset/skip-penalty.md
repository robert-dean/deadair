A record you cut short with Skip is now drawn less often for a while: a quarter of its usual chance at first, recovering evenly over a fortnight (both adjustable as "Play what you skip less often" in the rotation settings). Only your own Skip counts, from the console, an assistant or a chat command; a record cut because you disliked it, or at a change of programme, does not. It is a lean and never a refusal. Skips are kept in a new `track_skips` table, with how far into the record you skipped and who pressed it.
