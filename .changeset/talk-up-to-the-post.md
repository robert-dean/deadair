The presenter can talk up to the post. With `breaks.talkUp` on (off by default), a link short enough to finish before the singing starts on the next record is said over that record's intro instead of in the gap, landing its last word a moment before the first sung one. Where the singing starts comes from the record's timed lyrics, or from a correction on its page; a record with neither, an instrumental, or a link too long for the intro airs in the gap as before.
