A show's mix flow is easier to read and to change. On the show's page it is now a list of its rules in the order a record meets them, with a split's branches indented under it and any promise last, and a Block's bands, a Limit share's percent and when a Require falls due can be changed right there. On the canvas, select a node for **Bypass**, which switches a rule off without deleting it (the station holds nothing for it and tells the model nothing about it until you switch it back on, when it counts the whole show so far again), **Take its wires off** and **Remove**. Add a rule by typing to search for it, by double-clicking empty canvas, or by dropping a wire on empty canvas to see only the rules it can join. The canvas also gains a heading, a minimap, three kinds of wire and a strip saying what is wrong, and **Use this flow** waits until nothing would stop the flow running, now catching wires that go round in a circle and rules nothing reaches.
