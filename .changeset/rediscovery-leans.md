Two new leans on the draw from your own library, both on by default and both in the rotation settings. **Bring back artists you like**: an artist you have liked who has not aired for three weeks (adjustable) is drawn half again as often until they come back round. **Play deep cuts**: a record from an album you like, or with another record on it you like, that has not aired in the 120 days of history the station keeps is drawn half again as often, and the presenter is told it is an album track the station has not played in months. Both read your ratings and never how often the station has played something, and neither ever keeps a record off the air.
