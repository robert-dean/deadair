On a station set to a language other than English, a script reaches the voice without English words written into it. Numbers, times, symbols and abbreviations are left for the speech engine to read in its own language, instead of becoming "nineteen eighty-four" or "percent". Pronunciations mined from English articles are left out, while your own pronunciation entries still apply. A short lower-case stage direction such as `*lacht*` or `(seufzt)` is removed rather than read aloud.
