A show you add to the schedule over the current time no longer goes quiet for a couple of minutes when it starts. A show that is only a brief has its first records chosen ten minutes before it begins, but one added (or moved) so that it is already on had no such warning: the station switched to it at once with nothing to play and stayed silent while the records were chosen. The station now keeps the current show playing while it chooses the new one's opening records, and switches over as soon as they are ready, or after five minutes at the most.
