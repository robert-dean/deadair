A persona can be a **guest**: somebody who drops by the studio for a short chat with the host, rather than ringing in like a caller. A guest never presents the station, has no phrasings, and can be tied to the hosts whose shows they visit, as a caller is. The Personas page has New guest and lists guests in a group of their own. Refusing to put a character on air now says what kind it is, so a newsreader is no longer told it is a caller.
