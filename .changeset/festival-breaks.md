A festival show's talk breaks may talk about the festival: its name, its dates said as dates, where it is, who headlines, and the day an act on the record either side plays, all from what the station holds, and only while the festival is still to come or on when the break is heard. A sentence naming the festival with a date that is not one of its days, or putting an artist on its bill who is not on it, is refused and the break asked again.
