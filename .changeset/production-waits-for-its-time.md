A production asked for at a time now airs at that time. It used to go into the running order the moment it was finished, so a podcast queued for eleven at night aired whenever its last beat was joined, and one a `podcast` band on the station clock commissioned three hours ahead aired up to three hours early. A finished production with a time now waits, ready, for the first gap at or after it. The console's production form has an Air at field for it; left empty, a production airs as soon as it is made, as before.
