Breaks: a talk break that names an artist the way a presenter says it ("that Stones track", "White Stripes is up next", "that Petty track") is no longer refused as naming neither record. A band counts without its leading "The" and by the last word of its name, and a solo artist by surname, when the short form is capitalised and long enough to be a name.
