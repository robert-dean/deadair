A chart airing inside a scheduled block is cut to fit the time the block has left, keeping the top of the chart, so a countdown reaches number one before the next show starts instead of being cut off halfway. A one-hour block is roughly a top thirteen and a five-hour block most of a top seventy, from the same chart. A tenth of the time is held back for the host's talk when the show takes breaks. A station with no schedule still airs the whole chart.
