A break that back-announces a record whose title is six words or more and is sung in its own chorus, such as "Can't Get You out of My Head", is no longer refused for quoting the lyric. The lyric check now reads the break with the record names taken out, so naming the record is never counted as quoting it. A break that carries on from the title into the rest of the line is still refused.
