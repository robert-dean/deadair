A show built around a festival plays the acts on its bill, the ones played least recently first and headliners no more than anybody else, including acts your library does not hold yet. How much of each hour comes from the bill is the rotation setting "How much of a festival show comes from the festival's bill" (`rotation.festivalMix`, 60% by default, 0 turns it off); the rest of the hour follows the show's own brief.
