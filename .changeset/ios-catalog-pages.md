Record, album and artist pages, for a signed-in account, as on Android. A record's page shows its cover, credits, year and length, every time the station aired it, and what the enrichment providers say about it: tags, release details, the station's own claims with the source each came from, facts, a biography and links, and which provider said what and when. The credit opens the artist and the album opens the album, whose tracks open their records. Every record in Up next and in Played opens its page.
