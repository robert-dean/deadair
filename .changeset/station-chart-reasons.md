On a countdown of the station's own chart, the presenter is told why each record is there: how many times it aired on the station and how many times listeners asked for it over the time the chart covers, and whether the station likes it. A zero is left out. Only the station's own chart can say this: the same field is removed from anything a chart plugin answers, and the break guard counts the two figures as ones the break was shown.
