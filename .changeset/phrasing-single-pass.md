A phrasing now reads only its own placeholders. A record whose title or credit carried something that looked like one, such as `Song {{station.name}}`, aired with the station's name in its place when the phrasing named it inside an optional part, and one titled `Song {{station}}` quietly ruled out every phrasing that named it there. Titles now air exactly as written. A placeholder named after a built-in property, such as `{{toString}}` or `{{constructor}}`, is now reported as unknown in the console and the persona import preview instead of airing "[object Object]" or failing the break. The station's own phrasings say exactly what they said before.
