A show can keep its breaks dry, and its edit form shows its own sound. A schedule slot and a broadcast put on air by hand take `beds`: absent or true airs the station's written breaks over a music bed wherever the library has one for the show, false keeps them dry, and a rewrite that leaves it out keeps what the slot had, so an older app editing a show cannot turn its beds back on. The slot editor has a Sound section with that switch and a picker each for the jingles, idents and music beds that are the show's own, which sets each recording's scope as it is chosen.
